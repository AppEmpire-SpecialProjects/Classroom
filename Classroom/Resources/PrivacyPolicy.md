# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Classroom is a freemium schedule app for students and teachers, published by GCCY Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy describes what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. Classroom is designed local-first: the timetable you build stays on your device, and only the technical minimum needed to run the service ever leaves it.

## How Classroom stores your schedule

Your classes, homework, attendance marks, teaching role and preferences are saved in a local on-device database and system settings. There is no Classroom account, no sign-in screen and no schedule server that receives a copy of your week. Deleting a lesson, resetting the app in Settings, or uninstalling Classroom removes that information from the device.

## Technical information the app processes

When an online feature runs - loading subscription offers, completing a purchase, or receiving an updated paywall configuration - basic technical data is processed to make it work: your device's IP address, the version of the operating system, the parts of the app you open and how long a session lasts. Classroom does not access precise location, does not read other apps, and does not build advertising profiles. If you contact us by email, we use your address only to answer you, and occasionally to send essential service notices; marketing messages are opt-in.

## Who information can be shared with

Only aggregated, anonymized usage statistics may be reviewed to improve the app. Personal information is disclosed only in three situations: when we are required to by law, for example a subpoena or similar legal process; when we believe in good faith that disclosure is needed to protect someone's safety, investigate fraud or respond to a government request; and with trusted service providers that process data on our behalf - principally Apple (payments and subscriptions) and its infrastructure - bound by obligations no weaker than this policy.

## Payments and subscriptions

Classroom Premium is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the entire payment: we never see your card number or Apple ID credentials. To display live subscription state, the app works with the purchase platform Apphud, which receives only anonymized purchase and subscription status. You can view, change or cancel a subscription at any time in your App Store account settings.

## Calendar, reminders and backups

Class reminders and the optional morning summary are composed on your device and delivered through the system notification center. Calendar export writes recurring lessons into a separate Classroom calendar only after you grant access, and turning the feature off removes the exported events. A backup you create is a JSON file shared straight from the system share sheet to destinations you pick - it never travels through our servers.

## Deleting information and retention

We keep personal information only as long as the service needs it and for a reasonable period afterwards. Uninstalling Classroom stops all collection at once, using the standard uninstall options of your device or app marketplace. To request deletion of anything you have sent us through the app, email admin@gccyltd.com and we will handle it within a reasonable time.

## Children

Classroom is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. We ask children never to submit personal details through the app, and encourage parents and guardians to supervise its use and to instruct children accordingly. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, write to admin@gccyltd.com and we will take the appropriate action. Where local law requires, you must be at least 16 years old to consent to the processing of your personal information; in some places a parent or guardian may consent on your behalf.

## Security

We apply physical, electronic and procedural safeguards to the limited information the service processes, and design the app so that the most sensitive material - your actual schedule - never leaves the device at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version ships inside an app update and this page reflects it. Your continued use of Classroom after an update means you accept the current text. This policy is effective as of September 2026.

## Your consent

By downloading or using Classroom you agree to the processing described in this policy, as it stands today and as it is amended by us going forward.

## Contact

Questions about privacy while using the app, or about our practices? Email us at admin@gccyltd.com - a human reads every message.
